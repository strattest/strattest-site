strat-test

Helping traders develop and test their trading ideas.

Trading ideas are everywhere. They're in books and journals, on YouTube and all over social media. Scroll through a few charts and you can see some of them working, but that doesn't tell you how an idea really behaves over time.

illustration Tap to pause

strattest takes an idea back to its starting point, the thinking behind it, and works through it until every rule and every interpretation is written down and nothing is left to chance. Then the idea is tested under different market conditions, and the results are presented as they are.

How it works

  1. 01

    Your strategy

    However you trade it today.

  2. 02

    Exact rules

    Settled with you, every choice written down.

  3. 03

    The test

    Years of data, no hindsight.

  4. 04

    The results

    As they are, good or bad.

Tested strategies

Every test has its own repository with the notebook, the data and the results, so anyone can rerun it.
